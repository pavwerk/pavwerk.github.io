StockWatchPlus privacy policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

StockWatchPlus is a browser extension that shows stock prices and price alerts. This policy explains what it does with information.

Summary

StockWatchPlus does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising. The publisher, PavWerk, operates no server and receives nothing from your use of the extension.

What is stored on your device

The following is stored only in your browser profile on your device:

  • your watchlist (stock symbols), the share counts you enter, your price alerts and the history of alerts that triggered;
  • your settings (refresh interval, theme, color, notification and badge choices);
  • the most recent quotes and chart data, so the popup opens quickly.

You can remove all of it at any time by removing the extension. A backup file is created only if you choose "Export to file", and it is saved wherever you choose on your device.

What is sent over the internet

To show quotes, the extension requests data from Yahoo Finance (query1.finance.yahoo.com and query2.finance.yahoo.com). These requests contain the stock symbols on your watchlist and, when you search, the text you type in the search box. As with any web request, Yahoo receives your IP address. Your share counts, alerts and settings are never sent. No other server is contacted. Yahoo's handling of these requests is governed by Yahoo's own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you leave desktop notifications on, the extension asks your browser to display a notification when one of your price alerts is reached. Notifications are created on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • storage: to save the items listed above on your device.
  • alarms: to refresh quotes on the interval you choose.
  • notifications: to show price alert notifications.
  • Access to query1.finance.yahoo.com and query2.finance.yahoo.com: to fetch quotes, charts and search results.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
