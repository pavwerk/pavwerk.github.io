Zephyr privacy policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Zephyr is a browser extension that lets you control and watch your Cielo smart controllers (such as the Cielo Breez Max) from the browser toolbar. This policy explains what it does with information.

Summary

Zephyr does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no PavWerk account, no analytics and no advertising. The publisher, PavWerk, operates no server and receives nothing from your use of the extension.

What is stored on your device

The following is stored only in your browser profile on your device:

  • the Cielo API keys you add, with the names you give them;
  • the latest state of your units (name, temperature, humidity, mode, set temperature, fan, swing, preset) and what each unit can do;
  • the history of snapshots, kept for the number of days you choose (1, 7 or 30);
  • your settings (refresh interval, temperature unit, toolbar room, history length, theme).

Session tokens from Cielo are kept in the browser's session storage and are cleared when the browser closes. You can remove an account in Settings, and remove everything by removing the extension. Removing an account in Zephyr does not revoke the key; revoke it in the Cielo web portal.

What is sent over the internet

To show and change your units, Zephyr talks to Cielo's cloud service at api.smartcielo.com. Each request carries your Cielo API key and, for a change, the unit and the new setting. As with any web request, Cielo receives your IP address. No other server is contacted. Cielo's handling of this data is governed by Cielo's own privacy policy.

The sample rooms work entirely in your browser and send nothing.

Diagnostic info

The Copy diagnostic info button copies, to your clipboard only, what Cielo last sent about your units and the last failed command. It never includes your API key. Nothing is sent unless you choose to paste it into an email.

Permissions

  • storage: to save the items listed above on your device.
  • alarms: to refresh your units on the interval you choose, record history and update the toolbar badge.
  • Access to api.smartcielo.com: to read and change your Cielo units.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
