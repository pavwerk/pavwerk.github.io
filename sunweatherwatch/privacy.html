Sun & Weather Watch privacy policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Sun & Weather Watch is a browser extension that shows sunrise and sunset times, weather and air quality for places you choose. This policy explains what it does with information.

Summary

Sun & Weather Watch does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising. The publisher, PavWerk, operates no server and receives nothing from your use of the extension.

What is stored on your device

The following is stored only in your browser profile on your own device:

  • the locations you add (name and coordinates);
  • your settings (units, air quality index, clock format, refresh interval, badge choice);
  • the most recent forecast, so the popup opens quickly.

You can remove all of it at any time by removing the extension.

What is sent over the internet

To show forecasts, the extension requests data from Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, air-quality-api.open-meteo.com and geocoding-api.open-meteo.com). These requests contain the coordinates of the locations you saved and, when you search, the text you type in the search box. As with any web request, Open-Meteo receives your IP address. No other server is contacted. Open-Meteo's handling of these requests is governed by Open-Meteo's own privacy policy.

Your device's location

The extension does not ask for, read or use your device's location. It only knows the places you add yourself.

Permissions

  • storage: to save the items listed above on your device.
  • alarms: to refresh the forecast on the interval you choose and to update the toolbar badge at sunrise and sunset.
  • Access to api.open-meteo.com, air-quality-api.open-meteo.com and geocoding-api.open-meteo.com: to fetch forecasts and air quality and to look up places.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
